Privacy

Short version: this website does not track you.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no accounts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.
  • The trailer is a YouTube video in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only when you click it.
  • Links to Steam, Discord and social networks take you to those services, under their own policies.

The game

  • Settings, progress, stickers and your agreement to the terms of use are stored locally on your PC.
  • Online co-op uses Steam; Steam handles your account and matchmaking under Valve's privacy policy.
  • We do not sell personal data.
