SHORT DESCRIPTION
Up to four night workers count the stock of a hypermarket that rearranges itself when nobody is looking. Scan, carry, deliver the quota - and get out at six.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Night Stocktake is a co-op horror game about the worst night shift in retail. Clock in with up to three friends, grab a scanner and a cart, and count everything in a hypermarket that will not hold still: lights flicker, shelves rearrange, departments swap places the moment nobody is watching. Your tablet map is a snapshot that goes stale. Six things work the night with you - mannequins that move only when unseen, a book that bites, a shopper you must not scan near, a forklift that never brakes, a cashier who scans whoever stands in her lane and a guard who checks badges. Meet the quota, clock out at six, spend your wage on perk cards, stickers, heads, hats and scanner skins.

KEY FEATURES
- 1-4 player online co-op night shifts
- A store that rearranges itself when unobserved
- Physics: wobbly carts, falling goods, two-person fridges
- Six threats that hunt by noise, each in its own way
- 85 achievement stickers for your badge and your box head
- Box-head customization: heads, hats, shoes, faces, scanner skins, lockers
