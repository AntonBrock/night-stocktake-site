FAQ

How many players?

1 to 4 in online co-op. Solo works, but carrying a fridge alone is slow and loud.

Platforms and release date?

PC (Steam). Release date and price will be announced on Steam and our Discord - wishlist to get notified.

Is there voice chat?

Proximity voice is planned for the Steam release. Until then, use your favourite voice app.

How scary is it?

More creeping dread than gore: flickering lights, things that move when you look away, sudden loud noises. A photosensitivity warning and options to reduce flicker are in the game.

Can I stream it?

Yes, and you can monetize it. See the creator policy.

Languages?

English, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish and German: menus, HUD and subtitles.
